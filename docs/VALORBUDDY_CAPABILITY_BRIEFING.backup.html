THE VETERAN NEED

Veterans navigate transition across disconnected systems

01
Benefits and claims
Plain-language guidance is hard to find, and the application process is difficult to navigate.
02
Housing, credit, vehicles
Veteran-friendly options exist but are scattered across disparate sites and non-integrated programs.
03
Career and transition
Civilian job tools rarely translate military specialty codes (MOS, AFSC, Rating) and leadership experience well.
04
Community & belonging
Local veteran events, VSOs, and peer support networks are fractured and difficult to discover centrally.
Roughly 200,000 service members transition to civilian life each year. — Source: U.S. Government Accountability Office, GAO-24-107083 (2023).

RESPONSIBLE AI AND VETERAN SAFETY

Safety and privacy are designed into the core experience

Crisis-aware by design
Emergency and crisis guidance — 911 and Veterans Crisis Line (988, then press 1) — is permanently accessible in the top header of every screen.
Verified before delivery
A shared verification and safety layer checks every agent response against statutory sources before it reaches the Veteran.
Clear about its limits
A persistent notice states ValorBuddy is not a medical device, healthcare provider, or crisis replacement service.
Strict privacy boundaries
Third-party commercial partners never access private conversations, DD-214 documents, or sensitive profile fields.
