# ValorBuddy Marketplace Roadmap

## Product thesis

During active duty, trusted services and everyday necessities were organized around the service member. ValorBuddy can recreate that convenience after service by becoming one personalized, trusted front door connecting Veterans and military families to verified businesses, benefits, products, jobs and community resources.

## Phase 1 — Trust and measurable referrals

- Verified vendor profiles with Veteran-owned, military-friendly and accredited badges.
- Structured offers for vehicles, fuel, housing, insurance, travel, home services, education and employment.
- Veteran eligibility verification without sharing sensitive documents with vendors.
- Direct referral, appointment, quote and redemption actions.
- Privacy-safe vendor analytics: impressions, qualified referrals, redemptions and Veteran satisfaction.
- Veteran reviews with service-branch and verified-purchase signals, without exposing protected profile data.

## Phase 2 — Personalized marketplace intelligence

- A Marketplace Agent that matches needs, location, accessibility, budget and preferences to suitable offers.
- Life-event missions: separation, relocation, buying a vehicle, finding housing, starting school, launching a business or finding employment.
- Side-by-side offer comparison with transparent price, eligibility, terms and why each result was recommended.
- Veteran household access for spouses, caregivers and dependents.
- Saved preferences and renewal reminders for insurance, vehicle service, memberships and expiring offers.

## Phase 3 — Marketplace network

- Veteran-owned business storefronts and supplier discovery.
- Employer talent campaigns and skills-based Veteran matching.
- Sponsored access funded by brands, employers, foundations and local governments while core Veteran access remains free.
- API integrations for inventory, appointments, offers, fulfillment and referral status.
- Regional marketplace pilots followed by national category expansion.

## Revenue model

- Partner subscriptions for portal access, publishing and analytics.
- Qualified referral or completed-transaction fees where legally appropriate and clearly disclosed.
- Sponsored Veteran programs and category sponsorships.
- Enterprise licensing and API integrations.
- Optional promoted placement that is always visibly labeled and never overrides Veteran fit or safety.

## Trust rules

- No sale of private Veteran data.
- No vendor access to private conversations, uploaded documents, medical information or reminders.
- Clear labeling of sponsored results.
- Verified terms, direct official links and expiration dates.
- Veteran-controlled consent for every referral or data handoff.
- Human review and complaint handling for vendors and offers.

## Investor metrics

- Active Veterans and military-family households.
- Verified vendors and active offers by category and geography.
- Qualified-referral and redemption conversion rates.
- Repeat usage and cross-category adoption.
- Veteran savings delivered and time saved.
- Partner retention, subscription revenue and acquisition cost.
- Veteran satisfaction, complaint rate and trust score.
