# ValorBuddy Security Policy

Do not report vulnerabilities in public issues. Email `security@tagustechnologies.com` with the affected component, reproduction steps, impact, and any suggested mitigation. Do not include Veteran data, credentials, tokens, or production exports.

Tagus Technologies will acknowledge a report, triage its severity, preserve relevant audit evidence, and coordinate remediation. Authorization is required before testing production. Safe testing belongs in the isolated staging environment.

Security tooling and controls reduce risk; they do not by themselves constitute VA authorization, an ATO, FedRAMP authorization, or certification of compliance.
